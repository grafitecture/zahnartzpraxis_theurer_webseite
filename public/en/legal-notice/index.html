Professional title and chamber

Statutory professional title: Zahnärztin (dentist), conferred in the Federal Republic of Germany (licence to practise granted in Lower Saxony).

Competent chamber: Zahnärztekammer Niedersachsen (Lower Saxony Chamber of Dentists), Zeißstraße 11a, 30519 Hannover, www.zkn.de

Professional regulations

  • Act on the Practice of Dentistry (Zahnheilkundegesetz, ZHG)
  • Lower Saxony Act on the Chambers of the Healthcare Professions (Kammergesetz für die Heilberufe, HKG)
  • Professional Code of the Lower Saxony Chamber of Dentists (Berufsordnung der Zahnärztekammer Niedersachsen)
  • Scale of Fees for Dentists (Gebührenordnung für Zahnärzte, GOZ)

The ZHG and the GOZ are available at gesetze-im-internet.de, the HKG in the Lower Saxony legal information system (NI-VORIS), and the Professional Code on the website of the Lower Saxony Chamber of Dentists (www.zkn.de). All of them are available in German only.
