Further training and exchange in professional societies

  • German Society of Dental, Oral and Craniomandibular Sciences (DGZMK) The scientific umbrella organisation for dentistry in Germany, founded in 1859.
  • German Society of Paediatric Dentistry (DGKiZ) Professional society for the dental health of children, from birth onwards.
  • German Society of Periodontology (DG PARO) Professional society for healthy gums and firmly anchored teeth, with roots going back to 1924.
  • German Society of Oral Implantology (DGOI) Professional society for dental implants with practical further training, founded in 2004.
  • Academy of Practice and Science (APW) The DGZMK’s further training academy since 1974: knowledge from research for everyday practice.
