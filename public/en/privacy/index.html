2. Visiting the website and hosting

What data is processed

When you visit this website, your browser automatically sends information to the server. The following is processed:

  • the IP address of your device and the approximate location derived from it (such as the country),
  • the date and time of access,
  • the page or file requested, the status code and the amount of data transferred,
  • the previously visited page (referrer), if your browser sends it,
  • browser, operating system and technical characteristics of the connection.

Purpose and legal basis

This data is necessary to deliver the website, to ensure its stable operation and to fend off attacks. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest lies in providing the website securely and reliably. We do not analyse the data for statistics and do not combine it with other data.

Hosting with Vercel

The website is maintained by our web agency and hosted by Vercel Inc., 440 N Barranca Avenue #4133, Covina, CA 91723, USA. The agency processes the data on our behalf under a contract pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR; Vercel is engaged as a sub-processor (Vercel’s Data Processing Addendum).

Vercel delivers the pages via a worldwide server network, usually from a location near you. However, data is also processed in the USA, for example for logging, operations and security. According to Vercel, it additionally processes usage and metadata generated in the course of operating its services as a controller in its own right, in order to operate, secure and improve those services. The Vercel privacy policy applies to this processing.

Transfer to the USA

Vercel is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF). For certified companies, the European Commission has determined an adequate level of data protection (Art. 45 GDPR). In addition, Vercel has committed itself to the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses in its Data Processing Addendum (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). Nevertheless, it cannot be ruled out that US authorities may access data processed in the USA.

Protection against attacks

Vercel automatically checks incoming requests for attack patterns, for example on the basis of the IP address and technical characteristics of the connection. If traffic appears suspicious, Vercel may show a security check (“Vercel Security Checkpoint”) first. For this, your browser runs a verification script from Vercel that reads technical characteristics of the browser. Once the check has been passed, a verification session is stored in your browser for one hour (cookie) so that the check does not appear again. This is strictly necessary to fend off attacks (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Storage period

In our hosting account, access logs can be retrieved for no more than one day. Beyond that, according to Vercel, connection data is only stored for as long as necessary for operations, security and legal obligations.

3. Cookies, analytics and external content

We do not set any cookies and do not use comparable storage technologies such as local storage. The only exception is the security check described above in the event of suspicious traffic. We do not use any analytics, statistics, tracking or advertising services, including Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights. A cookie banner is therefore not necessary.

Fonts and the directions map are delivered together with the website. No connection is made to Google Fonts, Google Maps, OpenStreetMap or other third parties. The map is our own graphic based on OpenStreetMap data.

The preview images of our Instagram posts on the home page are also stored on our server. No connection to Instagram is made when the page loads (see section 6).

Links to other services, such as Google Maps, Apple Maps, WhatsApp, Instagram, the Chamber of Dentists or the emergency dental service, are only opened when you click them. You then leave our website, and the privacy policy of the respective provider applies.

4. Contact by phone, email or fax

If you call us or send us an email or fax, we process the information you provide, such as your name, contact details and your request, and possibly also information about your health. We use it to answer your enquiry, for example to arrange an appointment. In doing so, we are bound by professional confidentiality. We will explain to you in the practice how we process your data in the context of treatment, for example when billing via the Association of Statutory Health Insurance Dentists or when working with a dental laboratory.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR insofar as your treatment or its preparation is concerned, for health data in conjunction with Art. 9(2)(h) GDPR and Section 22(1) no. 1(b) BDSG (German Federal Data Protection Act). For other enquiries, we rely on our legitimate interest in answering them (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Emails are usually only encrypted in transit and are stored on the servers of our email service providers. For this reason, please do not send us findings, X-rays or other sensitive health data by email; talk to us instead.

We delete your enquiry as soon as it has been dealt with and no retention obligations apply. If it relates to your treatment, it becomes part of your patient record. We keep this for ten years after the end of treatment in accordance with Section 630f(3) BGB (German Civil Code), unless other regulations require otherwise.

5. Contact via WhatsApp

You can also write to us via WhatsApp. The provider is WhatsApp Ireland Limited, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, D04 X2K5, Ireland. When you write to us, WhatsApp processes, among other things, your phone number, your profile name, your messages and connection and usage data. Data is also transferred to WhatsApp LLC and other Meta companies in the USA; according to WhatsApp, it relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for this.

Using WhatsApp is voluntary; you can reach us just as well by phone or email. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR insofar as appointments or your treatment are concerned, for health data in conjunction with Art. 9(2)(h) GDPR and Section 22(1) no. 1(b) BDSG, and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Please do not send us findings, X-rays or other sensitive health data via WhatsApp. We only keep messages for as long as necessary for your request; anything relating to your treatment is transferred to your patient record.

Further information can be found in the WhatsApp privacy policy.

6. Our Instagram profile

We run a profile on Instagram: instagram.com/zahnarztpraxis_theurer. Instagram is provided by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, D04 X2K5, Ireland (“Meta”). There we share information about our practice, our team and dental health.

Preview images on this website

On our home page we show preview images of some posts. These images are stored on our own server and are plain links. No connection to Instagram is made when you visit the website, and no data is transferred to Meta. Instagram only opens in a new window when you click a preview image or a link to our profile.

When you visit our profile

When you visit our profile, Meta processes your data, for example your IP address, information about your device and browser and, if you are logged in, your usage behaviour. Meta uses cookies and similar technologies for this and also uses the data for its own purposes, such as advertising. We have no influence on this processing. Meta obtains consent for cookies and tracking on Instagram itself. Details can be found in the Instagram privacy policy.

Statistics (Insights)

Meta provides us with aggregated statistics about our profile, such as how many people have seen a post or roughly which region our visitors come from. We cannot see individual people. Insofar as we are jointly responsible with Meta for these statistics (Art. 26 GDPR), the agreement provided by Meta applies, the Page Insights Controller Addendum. In it, Meta assumes primary responsibility, including for responding to your requests. The essence of this agreement can be found in the information about Page Insights.

Comments and messages

If you follow us, comment on posts or like them, we see your profile name and your posts as Instagram displays them. We only use a direct message to deal with your request. Please do not send us findings, X-rays or other health data via Instagram; the best way to make an appointment is by phone.

Legal basis and transfer to the USA

We run the profile to provide information about our practice and dental health and to get in touch with you. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in this public relations work (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); where an appointment or your treatment is concerned, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Meta also transfers data to Meta Platforms, Inc. in the USA; the company is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Your rights

You can exercise your rights (section 9) against us and against Meta. Meta itself can usually help fastest, because only Meta has access to the user data. If you contact us, we will forward your request to Meta.

7. Job applications

If you apply to us, by email or by post, we process your details and documents in order to decide on employment. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Your documents are only seen by those people in the practice who are involved in the selection.

If you are not employed, we delete your data no later than six months after the end of the application process. This period serves as evidence in the event of possible legal disputes (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We only keep your documents for longer if you have consented to this (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). If you are employed, your documents are placed in your personnel file.

8. Recipients of your data

We only pass on your data if this is necessary for the purposes stated, if you have consented or if there is a legal obligation to do so. Recipients may be:

  • our web agency, which maintains the website, and Vercel as hosting provider (section 2),
  • IT and telecommunications service providers, for example for phone, fax and email (section 4),
  • WhatsApp, if you write to us via WhatsApp (section 5),
  • Meta, if you visit our Instagram profile or write to us there (section 6).

9. Your rights

You have the right to

  • access to the data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) and restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR), unless retention obligations prevent this,
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • withdraw any consent you have given, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

To do so, simply contact us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is the State Commissioner for Data Protection of Lower Saxony (Landesbeauftragter für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen), Prinzenstraße 5, 30159 Hannover, www.lfd.niedersachsen.de.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)

Where we process data on the basis of our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you may object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then no longer process the data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

10. Further information

Encryption

The connection to this website is encrypted using TLS. You can recognise this by “https://” in the address bar and the padlock symbol in your browser.

Obligation to provide data, no automated decision-making

You do not have to provide any information to visit the website; however, it cannot be displayed without the technically necessary connection data. For an appointment or a job application, we need the information required for this, otherwise we cannot deal with your request. There is no automated decision-making, including profiling (Art. 22 GDPR).

Changes

We will amend this privacy policy if the website or the legal situation changes. The version published here applies.
